**Identity tag:** government_shift
Route here when you need to query **shift scheduling, staff assignments, service delivery coordination, customer interaction tracking, or operational analytics** for a public-sector or government service organization.
Hard negatives: This is NOT a real-estate or property database (no lot/land records), NOT a retail store inventory system (no product SKUs or merchandising), NOT a drama/arts workshop enrollment system, and NOT a general customer address book without service-delivery context.
Use this database for government workforce management, multi-channel service request routing, and interaction history tied to shift operations—not for civic records, retail transactions, or community group administration.
