Headphone retail inventory database. Includes headphones with model, class, driver-matched dB, construction, earpads, and price, plus stores with neighborhood, parking, opening date, and stock quantities by store. Route here for questions about the most expensive headphone, the most common headphone class, distinct models, stock levels, or which stores carry a model. Distinct from broader e-commerce datasets because it is a compact store-and-stock retail catalog for headphones.
