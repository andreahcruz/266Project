Address-centric customer order tracking. Primary distinguishing features are postal/customer addresses, address details, product type codes, category-like product questions such as Clothes or Hardware, and payment method codes actually used by customers. Route here for address counts and address lookups, product catalog questions with type codes or Clothes/Hardware categories, price-by-product-type questions, and payment method code usage by customers. Unlike the broader e-commerce DB, this one is not about invoices, shipments, tracking numbers, or shipped items.
