Full e-commerce platform — the canonical online shopping database. Customers, products, orders, and order items are all here. Routes any question about: customer purchase history, products they bought, order quantities, items per order, total spend, customer-to-order relationships, repeat buyers, or aggregate sales by customer or product. Handles the common e-shop queries that involve customers, orders, and products. Also tracks invoices, shipments with tracked items, and stored payment methods, but most queries focus on customers + their orders + the products bought.
